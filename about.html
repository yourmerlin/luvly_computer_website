Merlin Susana Enriquez


creative tech + hci + design @ carnegie mellon university


I am a creative technologist, researcher, and educator working at the intersection of art, textiles, and technology. My research focuses on soft electronics, holding people's data with care, and creating open-source programs that build pathways into STEAM (science, technology, engineering, art, and mathematics). Right now, I'm sewing circuits onto fabric, using 3D printers to create intricate batik patterns, and disguising technology within fashion pieces ୭˚. ᵎᵎ


My conceptual practice focuses on missing or absent data, misusing surveillance technology, and creating playful websites. Some of my work is rooted in activism, and some of it is just about making technology feel silly, playful, and unserious. I wholeheartedly believe play is a form of resistance. There has to be a balance between enjoying life and combating the systems that restrict that enjoyment!


Outside of my research and practice, I am a lifestyle lolita. Wearing lolita isn't just a fashion statement but a political one, too. It disrupts environments that force you to conform. Lolita is my armour (..◜ᴗ◝..)!
